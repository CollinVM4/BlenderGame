# First smoothie tutorial recovery

Guide players to the next step of their first delivery using the server's current order, inventory, and blender state.

- Start with the normal Red order and stash hint. Three carried ingredients lead to the ladder, including a mix gathered at the market. A loose throw or unrelated tool alone never advances the learner past the ladder.
- Once ingredients are accepted into the blender, guide the player to finish loading, spin, or dispense as appropriate. A valid carried cup already prepared by the player goes straight to the serve hint after taking the order.
- The first valid smoothie served to the Red tutorial customer advances the tutorial and pays at least $30,000 once, including C and D grades. Show the actual grade and customer reaction. The next customer is Cold.
- Keep the existing upgrade and market guidance after the first delivery. Purchased upgrades count toward that guidance when it appears.

## Playtest

- Gather ingredients at the market before taking the first order. With three ingredients, the next hint is the ladder. With a batch already loaded, the next hint is the turbine.
- Serve an off-recipe first smoothie. Confirm that the customer shows the grade, the payout reaches $30,000, and the queue proceeds to Cold.
